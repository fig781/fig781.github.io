# Duelfront privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Duelfront is a game that runs entirely on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing.
Duelfront collects no personal data and no usage data, and it shares nothing with anyone.
It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.
It doesn't ask for the internet permission, so it can't send anything off your device.

## What stays on your device

The armies you build and your sound settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device, so they are there the next time you play.
They never leave your device.
Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

## Children

Duelfront is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.
